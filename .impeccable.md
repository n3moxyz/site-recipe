## Design Context

### Users

First-time and non-designer website builders, often moving quickly in a hackathon or creative sprint. They have an idea but lack vocabulary for page structure, UI patterns, motion, and access. Their job is to leave with a coherent brief they can paste into ChatGPT Sites in under two minutes.

### Brand Personality

Clear, curious, and enabling. The interface should replace blank-page anxiety with momentum and quiet confidence. It teaches through concrete choices without sounding academic or patronizing.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light-first maker's field guide: crisp workshop typography, tactile catalog cards, visible guide lines, saturated blue for decisions, coral for emphasis, and restrained motion. References include Name That UI, shadcn/ui, Motion Sites, Awwwards, and the Manus Creator Program. Avoid generic AI gradients, glossy dashboard chrome, award-site spectacle, and copied reference assets.

### Design Principles

- Put a useful choice in the first viewport; do not make people read a pitch before starting.
- Teach by letting people choose real ingredients, then reflect every choice in a live brief.
- Use plain language first and introduce design vocabulary only when it helps someone prompt more precisely.
- Keep each catalog deliberately small; curation is part of the product's value.
- Make keyboard focus, contrast, responsive behavior, and reduced motion part of the baseline.
